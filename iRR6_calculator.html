Ruxolitinib underdosing relative to platelet count
Dose lower than prescribing indication based on PLT count at any time point (baseline, 3 months, or 6 months). Variable with highest prognostic impact. Underdosing is often driven by anemia concerns; however, dose relative to PLT count — not absolute dose — carries prognostic significance.

Absence of spleen reduction ≥50% at 6 months (no SR50)
No ≥50% reduction in palpable spleen length from baseline at month 6. Includes: no SR50 at months 3 and 6; or SR50 achieved at month 3 but lost at month 6. Note: SR30 threshold (used in original RR6) loses prognostic value specifically in INT-1 patients, who respond better to ruxolitinib; SR50 is the appropriate cutoff in this subgroup.

Clinical Disclaimer. This tool is intended exclusively for qualified healthcare professionals as a decision-support aid. The iRR6 model was derived from retrospective multicenter data (RUX-MF; n = 428 INT-1 patients) and validated externally (Moffitt Cancer Center; n = 95). Molecular data were not systematically incorporated (NGS unavailable in a significant proportion of the original cohort). Spleen response was assessed by palpation; inter-observer variability is an acknowledged limitation. This calculator does not substitute clinical judgment, institutional protocols, or multidisciplinary discussion. Risk stratification should always be interpreted in the context of the individual patient's full clinical picture, comorbidities, performance status, and preferences regarding transplantation.
